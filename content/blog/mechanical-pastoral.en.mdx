This portfolio is not an empty scaffold and not a product landing. The direction is **Mechanical Pastoral**: mechanical type (EfT Mecanical), one dithered surface as the anchor, and quiet ASCII on top — no cards in the hero, no purple glow, no invented CV.

I took mood from references (SF Compute dither, dark typographic portfolios) and dropped the marketing chrome: cloud sidebars, pills, stats. The first viewport should still feel like me without the nav: Bold name, one headline, one support line, one CTA group, one visual anchor.

The code lives on GitHub. Draft posts use `draft: true` and leave the index and RSS in production. PT is primary; EN stays in parity.

If bio or dates are off, open a PR or tell me. I would rather correct than invent.
